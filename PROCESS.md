# Process: How I used AI for this assignment

## Tools used
I used the Doubao AI assistant for this assignment. All core viewpoints and ideas in the essay are my own. I used AI to support brainstorming, content organisation and document formatting.

When I received Dylan Beattie’s one‑hour talk video, I did not watch it from start‑to‑finish straight away. Instead, I sent the video link to Doubao and asked for a summary of its main theme. From the summary I quickly grasped the central question: whether code can be regarded as art. With this preliminary background, I then watched the original video myself and paid close attention to its real‑world examples of code as art.

After watching the talk, I pasted all my scattered, unorganised thoughts into the chat as one big block of text. I only asked Doubao to reorganise my wording. It turned my rough notes into well‑structured paragraphs. I double‑checked carefully to make sure the reorganised text faithfully represented what I wanted to say.

## One piece of AI‑generated content I kept
I kept the well‑structured paragraph layout and polished sentences produced from my raw thoughts. I also used the HTML styling code it created to adjust font size, colour and italics for the title, note section and bibliography inside README.md. These are only formal improvements and did not change my original arguments.

## Content produced by AI that I rejected
At an early stage, with little input from me, I asked AI to write a full first draft. That version spent most of its space simply retelling content from the video. It summarised what the speaker said rather than developing my own independent analysis. I considered this draft not suitable for the assignment, so I discarded it entirely and rewrote the essay based fully on my personal reflection.

## Overall summary
Doubao worked as a brainstorming helper, language organiser and formatting tool. All critical judgements and perspectives belong to me. I evaluated and filtered AI‑generated output, and every piece of surviving material was checked by myself.
